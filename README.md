# evenor

Tell the story of your own life.

evenor is a plain-text database editor for people who want full
control over their data. Your records live as flat CSV files on
your device. No cloud, no account, no vendor lock-in.

<!-- screenshot: main view with a few records -->

## Design

- **Offline-first.** Everything lives on your filesystem.
- **Plain-text.** Open your data in any text editor.
- **Omnipresent.** Web, macOS, Linux, Windows, Android, iOS.
- **Naive.** A child should be able to understand the internals.

## What it looks like

Records are interactive sentences you can expand, collapse,
and navigate. Click a value to focus on it and see everything
connected to it.
